# Localization and accessibility

All new product copy is generated from English and Arabic ARB resources. Home,
scanner, membership, operation, reward, recovery, blocked states, Device &
Security, Settings, and App Lock have RTL-aware layouts.

Review evidence covers Arabic Home, Arabic 5/8 customer, Arabic reward/redeem,
dark Home, 200% Home, and 200% customer screens. Scanner controls have semantic
labels and touch targets. Stamp progress is one semantic group (“5 of 8
stamps”), while visible slots remain only FILLED or EMPTY.

Physical VoiceOver/TalkBack traversal and platform reduced-motion behavior
remain hardware checks.
