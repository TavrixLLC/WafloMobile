# Accessibility

- Primary controls meet a 48 logical-pixel minimum.
- The stamp grid exposes one concise progress summary instead of announcing
  every slot.
- FILLED/EMPTY states differ by shape/fill, not color alone.
- Scanner frame, live status, flash, close, permission recovery, operation
  recovery, success, and App Lock expose semantic labels/live regions.
- 200% Home and Customer renders remain scrollable with no clipped action.
- Dark themes retain high-contrast text, borders, scanner status, and stamps.
- Motion is short and informational; haptics are optional, never the only cue.
