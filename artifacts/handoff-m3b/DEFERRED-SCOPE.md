# Deferred scope

- Merchant artwork bytes/URL: the approved M2 contract exposes FILLED/EMPTY
  digests but no retrievable payload. M3B uses a strong two-state fallback and
  does not fabricate artwork delivery.
- Physical camera, flash, biometric, app-switcher, and one-handed verification.
- Android/iOS store signing credentials and distribution.
- Live staging endpoint, real merchant/location pairing authority, and
  cross-device Customer Web/Wallet observation.
- Human identity integration until the Web/Backend-owned contract is approved.
- Notification delivery, merchant administration/reporting, Wallet issuance,
  NFC, Smart Tap, POS, and offline mutations.

No deferred item requires broadening Staff Mobile to begin implementation-free
physical staging E2E once the external environment and devices are available.
