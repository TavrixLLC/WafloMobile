# Accessibility

Brand integration preserved or improved:

- minimum 48px operational touch targets;
- high-contrast White on Brick and Warm Ink on Coral/Soft Coral;
- semantic severity labels in addition to color;
- concise grouped stamp-progress semantics such as “5 of 8 stamps”;
- FILLED/EMPTY loyalty state without a color-only reward state;
- readable scanner controls and status copy;
- RTL focus and reading order;
- 200% Home, customer, and manager-approval screenshots;
- safe fallback palette when a ThemeExtension is unavailable;
- official marks exposed as a single image semantic, not duplicated child semantics;
- reduced-motion-compatible 160–240ms transitions with no looping brand animation.

The visual review found no clipped dominant action in the required large-text evidence. Physical TalkBack/VoiceOver verification remains an owner/device step.
