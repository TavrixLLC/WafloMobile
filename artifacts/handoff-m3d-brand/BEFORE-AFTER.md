## First review findings and refinements

1. Prior pine/green visual authority made the app feel disconnected from the official commercial identity. It was remapped to the official Cloud/White/Ink/Brick/Coral system.
2. Startup and configuration failure looked generic. They now use the official mark, Warm Ink trust surface, and restrained flow-status motif.
3. Arabic fixtures did not fully demonstrate native operational context. Merchant/location fixture content was localized for the Arabic review.
4. Brand presence risked becoming repetitive. Official marks were limited to trust transitions; daily operational screens rely on type, color, and spacing.
5. A manually approximated mark existed as a fail-safe layer. It was removed; the official asset is normal authority and a separate three-dot status motif is the non-logo fallback.

## Second review conclusion

- Next action remains obvious on Home, success, recovery, and approval screens.
- Scanner remains camera-first with minimal opaque coverage.
- Customer identity, progress, and action hierarchy are immediate.
- Reward-ready remains outside a completely FILLED grid.
- Final redemption still shows 0/goal and all EMPTY.
- Arabic, dark mode, and 200% evidence remain coherent.
- Screens no longer read as a generic Material demo, while product behavior remains unchanged.
