# Scanner accessibility

- Scan target and statuses expose semantic labels/live-region updates.
- Torch and close controls have labels and retail-sized targets.
- Status is visible; haptics are supplementary.
- `disableAnimations` renders a static target and never starts the beam.
- English, Arabic RTL, dark camera treatment, and 200% text have executable screenshots.
- QR-frame geometry does not mirror semantically in RTL; copy/alignment and directional navigation do.
- Permission-denied and permanent-denial states distinguish Try Again from Open Settings.
