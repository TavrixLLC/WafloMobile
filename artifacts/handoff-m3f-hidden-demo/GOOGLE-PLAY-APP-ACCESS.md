# Google Play App Access

Waflo Staff normally requires merchant-authorized device pairing. Review access is limited to a server-isolated tenant with fictional sample data.

1. Launch the app.
2. Open the normal scanner from setup.
3. Tap **Enter code instead**.
4. Enter `<REVIEW_ACCESS_CODE_FROM_SECURE_RELEASE_CONFIG>` from the secure Play Console access instructions.
5. Continue.

The production app does not contain a master credential or local authorization decision. Once authorized, the Demo indicator and fixed Review Tools scenarios become visible. Reset and exit actions affect only the isolated review environment.
