# RTL and accessibility

Verified presentation evidence includes English, Arabic RTL, light, dark,
native 2.0× text scaling, and reduced motion.

## Implemented protections

- Logical start/end padding and alignment.
- Direction-aware navigation and chevrons.
- Manual technical credential entry remains LTR where required.
- Responsive wrapping instead of ellipsis for operational labels.
- Summary rows switch to vertical reflow at large text.
- Choice groups switch from horizontal to vertical at large text.
- Primary CTA icons yield space at large text rather than splitting words.
- Scrollable App Lock, Settings, Security, and operation screens keep actions
  reachable at 200%.
- Scanner Reduce Motion presents a static semantic target.
- Practical 48+ touch targets, semantics, live regions, and status meaning are
  retained from the existing product and shared design primitives.

Only English and Arabic authoritative localization sources exist in the
starting product. Sorani and Badini copy was not invented and remains pending
an authoritative source.

Automated evidence does not replace assistive-technology and owner physical
review on target hardware.
