# Product overview

Waflo Staff is the merchant-side mobile tool for in-person loyalty operations. It should feel warm, calm, immediate, and trustworthy—not like an ERP, analytics dashboard, bank, or developer console.

The dominant loop is short: scan a customer's Waflo code, read authoritative membership state, add stamps or redeem an eligible reward, show the committed result, and scan the next customer.

Merchant administration, customer Wallet issuance, reports, notifications, POS, NFC, and offline mutations are outside this Mobile product.
