# Design rules

- Make the next Staff action obvious in under one second.
- Preserve a camera-first scanner and one-handed retail ergonomics.
- Use official Waflo color and typography tokens; do not approximate the logo.
- Prefer whitespace and hierarchy over stacked cards.
- Use large touch targets, concise copy, and restrained motion.
- Do not expose technical identifiers or security mechanics in normal UI.
- Arabic is true RTL; credential/code values remain LTR-safe.
- Dark mode is designed, not inverted.
- Progress is accessible without adding a third visual stamp state.
- Never recolor merchant-owned artwork merely to match Waflo chrome.
