# Accessibility

- Minimum 48 logical-pixel scanner controls and practical retail touch targets elsewhere.
- Live semantic scanner status; haptics are supplementary.
- Stamp progress announces “5 of 8 stamps” while the visible grid remains two-state.
- Support 200% text without obscuring primary actions.
- Preserve focus order, readable contrast, safe areas, and screen-reader grouping.
- Respect Reduce Motion.
- Use text and iconography in addition to color for success, warning, and error.
- Keep manual-code values LTR within an RTL shell.
