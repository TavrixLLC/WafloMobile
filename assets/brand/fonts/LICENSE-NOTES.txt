Bundled typography
==================

Manrope and Noto Sans Arabic are open-source typefaces licensed under the
SIL Open Font License 1.1.

Official project sources:
- https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/manrope
- https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/notosansarabic

The bundled Noto Sans Arabic file is the official variable width/weight build,
renamed only to avoid platform-hostile bracket characters in the asset path.
Its source filename is NotoSansArabic[wdth,wght].ttf and its SHA-256 is
63111b5b2e074dd48cc67692e0a2726d86ee94c1c37fe8598257b7b4e87e869e.

The selected family names and weights are prescribed by the read-only Waflo
Brand System package. The font files are bundled with Waflo Staff so the
application never depends on runtime font downloads.

The complete upstream license texts are bundled beside the fonts as
Manrope-OFL.txt and NotoSansArabic-OFL.txt.
